# Hot-Weather Operations Policy

**DEMO DOCUMENT** — fictional policy created for the Cat Track hackathon.

Applies to: all machines on job sites where the forecast high is 35 C or above. Effective immediately at North Ridge Quarry and Harbor Logistics Park.

## Rules

1. Operators must idle the engine for 5 minutes before shutdown after any loaded climb.
2. Any coolant temperature warning must be reported in Cat Track within 15 minutes, with the unit ID.
3. Radiator and cooler cores on Off-Highway Trucks must be blown out every 100 hours while this policy is in effect.
4. Cat 777 haul trucks must not make more than 3 consecutive loaded ramp climbs without a 10 minute cool-down at idle.
5. Do not open a radiator cap on a hot engine.
6. Site managers must post a spotter at blind corners on haul roads during shift change.
7. Operators are required to take a 10 minute shade break every 2 hours; supervisors must log breaks.

## Why

Field reports from North Ridge Quarry show Cat 777 trucks overheating on loaded ramp climbs in dusty, 40 C+ conditions (CID 110 FMI 15). The radiator-cleaning quick fix works best when done more often than the standard 250 hour interval.
